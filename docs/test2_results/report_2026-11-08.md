## Things that looked wrong, inconsistent with Amendments 26 to 36, or ambiguous

1. **Missing-P counts are not recorded as counts.** Amendment 28 decision 14 asks for "the first-half reconciliation and missing-P counts". reconciliation.json records only `missing_P_games_checked` (1,767 per table, 54 tables). The count of missing-P games, 0, is implied by the passed stop check and is not written anywhere. Whether this meets decision 14 needs a decision. This is a recording gap, not a failed check.
2. **"Near-tie" in steps 2 and 3 is ambiguous.** Amendment 26 decision 5 defines a tie only against the best option. The ladder and key-starter steps use a one-sided "beats by more than the tolerance" rule. My section 4 counts treat |mean diff| ≤ SE as a near-tie there. If you meant "not kept" instead, the counts in that column are the same in this run (2, 2, 2, 2, 1, 2 and 2). Every comparison that was not kept also has |mean diff| ≤ SE, and none is worse by more than SE.
3. **Step-1 comparison and tie counts include the best compared with itself** (one of the 54 entries, mean diff 0, SE 0, tied true). Amendment 26 decision 9 says "at most 57 configurations are compared". The file's 54 + 2 + 1 = 57 fits that only if the best's self-entry counts.
4. **The 2023 rush term was kept by a narrow margin** (mean diff 0.24833722245422757 against SE 0.22734450165978135). As a result, 2023 alone uses the full run, while 2021, 2022, 2024 and the final choice use no_qb_rush. This follows the rule as written. I note it as a fact, not as an error.
5. **Large tied sets in 2019 (30 of 54) and 2020 (32 of 54)** mean those choices fell back to the default point. This matches Amendment 26 decision 11's known limit for early seasons.
6. **The timing rerun estimate differs from Amendment 36.** The rerun's upper-bound estimate is 60.84867596626282 seconds against Amendment 36's 56 seconds. Load time is 1.6394569873809814 against 1.41, and the fit times are 0.01498, 0.01195, 0.01297 and 0.01231 seconds. Amendment 36 decision 3 allows fit times to differ.

7. **2018 lookbacks 5 and 8 give identical errors** for all 162 pairs in errors_by_season.csv, as Amendment 27 decision 9 expects. This is not an error.
8. **The pooled default-vs-walk-forward comparison in section 5 has no SE.** It is descriptive only. Test 2 makes no judgement from it.
9. The five modified data/raw/kalshi_lines_*.csv files in the working tree are unrelated to test 2. They were not touched.
